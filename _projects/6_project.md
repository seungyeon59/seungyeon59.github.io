Built in 24 hours at **SteelHacks XIII** (University of Pittsburgh, Sept 19–20, 2026), then extended into a workshop paper.

[Demo Video](https://youtu.be/aSscBtHs2c8) · [Live Demo](https://once-upon-a-play.vercel.app/) · [Slides](https://github.com/seungyeon59/once-upon-a-play/blob/main/docs/assets/SteelHacks_Presentation.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/seungyeon59/once-upon-a-play)

AI doesn't have to replace a child's creativity. It can give that creativity somewhere to go. Once Upon a Play is an interactive storybook game for children aged 9–12 that turns them from readers into story authors, through exploration and choice.

- **Three playable tales:** Little Red Riding Hood, Snow White, and Cinderella, on a draggable 2.5D map with parallax scrolling.
- **Character dialogue:** Scripted lines combined with Claude-generated responses.
- **"What happens next?":** Children ask for a custom scene in natural language, and the story continues from it.
- **Drawing scanner:** Hand-drawn sketches become characters in the story.
- **Narration and keepsake:** ElevenLabs text-to-speech, plus a printable PDF storybook.

Our design insight was that "exploration first" engagement and near-zero latency are what keep children immersed.

**Tech:** React, Zustand, Pixi.js, Express, Anthropic Claude, ElevenLabs, Vercel

**Team Steel Hungry:** Chiyoung Kim, Sumin Shim, Doyoung Heo, Seungyeon Back
