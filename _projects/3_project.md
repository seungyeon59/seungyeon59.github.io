An empirical study proposing a novel trajectory similarity measure for irregular spatio-temporal GPS data.

- Proposed a novel trajectory similarity measure integrating spatial, temporal, and velocity components for irregular spatio-temporal data.
- Designed an ε-ball-based spatial similarity metric and a data-adaptive weighting framework (α, β, γ) for flexible similarity modeling.
- Applied hierarchical clustering to large-scale GPS trajectory data to uncover latent behavioral patterns.
